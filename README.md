# LASC Internal Management System

A web-based internal management system developed during my internship at **LASC in Los Angeles**.

The system was created to digitize and improve administrative workflows related to **teacher scheduling, payroll-related time tracking, and multi-campus book inventory management**.

---

## Overview

During my internship, I worked with staff to understand recurring administrative tasks and identify areas where manual workflows could be improved.

Based on those needs, I designed and developed an internal system that supports:

- Teacher schedule management
- Teaching-hour tracking for payroll-related work
- Book inventory management
- Inventory transfers between campuses
- Search, filtering, and data validation

This project was built around actual operational needs rather than a predefined academic assignment.

---

## Business Problem

Several day-to-day administrative tasks were managed manually or across separate workflows.

Key challenges included:

- Difficulty checking teacher schedules and working hours quickly
- Repetitive manual review of information used for payroll-related tasks
- Managing book inventory across multiple campuses
- Tracking stock-in, stock-out, and inter-campus transfers
- Preventing inconsistent or invalid inventory records

These challenges created opportunities to improve accessibility, consistency, and efficiency through a centralized digital system.

---

## Solution

I designed and implemented a web-based internal management system that combines scheduling and inventory functions in one place.

The system translates operational requirements into practical workflows that staff can use for daily administrative tasks.

### Main Modules

#### Teacher Scheduling

- Manage teacher schedules and class information
- Track teaching and working hours
- Support payroll-related time review
- Manage additional hours and class status
- Organize scheduling information in a centralized view

#### Book Inventory Management

- Manage inventory across multiple campuses
- Record book stock-in and stock-out
- Transfer inventory between campuses
- Search and filter inventory records
- Prevent invalid operations such as negative inventory
- Maintain chronological inventory transaction records
